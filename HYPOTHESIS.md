Rule: After a jobs report, CPI release, or FOMC statement, if the Polymarket
Dec-decision odds move 2+ points between the release and minute 2,
bet on a reversal at minute 2 and exit at minute 30.

Test set: all qualifying releases after 2026-11-20 (not yet observed).
Success: positive average P&L after 1 point of round-trip costs.
This rule will not be changed after this commit.
